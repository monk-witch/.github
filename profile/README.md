# The Monk & The Witch

*The Monk & The Witch* is an original, character-driven **dark fantasy saga** by [Fred Potvin](https://monkandwitch.com/en/author/), set on **Mythic Earth**—a reimagined Earth where familiar cities and forests hold dangerous magic.

The story follows **Rishi**, a human monk determined to answer violence with mercy, and **Maeril**, a quick-witted witch who shapes wards against danger. Their relationship shapes a saga about love, practical compassion, and the consequences of trying to help in a world too large to save.

Begin with *Mercy and Wards*, the first book. A monk takes a blade meant for a frightened boy. A witch watching through her hawk refuses to let his mercy end in a prison cell.

**[Start with Book 1 — Mercy and Wards. Read free online.](https://monkandwitch.com/en/b1/)**

*For mature readers.*
